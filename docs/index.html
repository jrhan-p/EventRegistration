RCCC Community Events

Online registration, digital tickets, and check-in for our church community's gatherings, conferences, and meals.

✦ How it works

1 · RegisterSign up online for an event in about a minute.
2 · Get your ticketA QR ticket arrives by email — add it to Apple Wallet or Google Wallet.
3 · Check inShow your code at the door and at meal pickup. That's it.

About

RCCC Community Events is the event registration and ticketing system for Rutgers Community Christian Church activities. Registration links are shared for each event; tickets are personal QR codes delivered by email and saved to your phone's wallet.

Questions about an event or your registration? Reply to your confirmation email and an organizer will help you.
